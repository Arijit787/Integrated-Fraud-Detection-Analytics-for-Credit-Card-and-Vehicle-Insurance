# Integrated Fraud Detection Analytics for Credit Card & Vehicle Insurance

## Project Overview

Developed an integrated fraud analytics solution using Power BI, Python, and SQL to analyze credit card fraud and vehicle insurance claims, identify fraud patterns, and support data-driven risk assessment.

The project combines data preparation, database management, exploratory analysis, and interactive dashboards to transform fraud-related data into actionable business insights.

## What This Dashboard Helps Analyze

- **Credit Card Fraud:** Examine fraud amounts, contract types, income categories, and customer characteristics to explore patterns in fraudulent transactions.
- **Vehicle Insurance Fraud:** Analyze fraud cases by policy type, fault category, vehicle manufacturer, gender, and claim characteristics.
- **Fraud Trends:** Compare monthly fraud patterns to identify variations that may warrant further investigation.
- **Claim & Regional Analysis:** Explore claim amounts, regional ratings, and weekday-based claim activity to understand differences in insurance claim patterns.
- **Risk Indicators:** Examine customer, transaction, policy, and claim attributes to support further fraud investigation.

## Business Value

The solution provides a consolidated view of fraud-related metrics, helping decision-makers:

- Identify patterns and anomalies that warrant further investigation.
- Compare fraud trends across credit card and vehicle insurance datasets.
- Monitor fraud-related metrics through interactive dashboards.
- Support risk assessment and data-informed fraud prevention strategies.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **DAX:** Measures and KPI calculations
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and preparation
- **Python (Pandas):** Data cleaning and analysis
- **SQL (MySQL):** Database management and data querying
- **Excel:** Data preparation and supporting analysis
